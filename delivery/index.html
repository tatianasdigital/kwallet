Shipping

Need help with your delivery? Get in touch with us at kampela@demo.fi.

Shipping from €10

Delivered from Finland within 5-12 business days on average

Your Kampela device is prepared and shipped from Finland. We offer international shipping, so you can order your device wherever you are. Below you’ll find everything you need to know about shipping options, delivery times, and tracking your order.

Delivery details

We offer worldwide shipping. Available delivery options depend on your destination and are displayed during checkout. If your country is not available at checkout, please contact us at kampela@demo.fi and we'll help you find a solution.

Shipping costs depend on your delivery address and the available shipping methods. The exact price will be displayed at checkout before you complete your purchase. International shipping starts from €10.

Delivery times depend on your destination and the shipping method selected at checkout. Orders are dispatched from Finland, and international deliveries may take longer due to customs processing or local postal services.

You'll receive an estimated delivery timeframe when placing your order.

If tracking is available for your selected shipping method, you'll receive a tracking number once your order has been dispatched. You can use this number on the shipping carrier's website to check the status of your delivery.

International orders may be subject to customs duties, import taxes, or other fees depending on the destination country. These charges are determined by local authorities and are not necessarily included in the product or shipping price. Please check your country's import regulations before placing an order.

If your package hasn't arrived within the estimated delivery timeframe, first check its tracking information for the latest updates. If there are no updates or you believe your package may be lost, contact us at kampela@demo.fi with your order number. We'll help you investigate the issue with the shipping provider.
