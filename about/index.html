About us

Finland has a long tradition of engineering things differently. We believe good technology should be accessible, transparent, and built to last. Kampela is our take on a hardware wallet: simple on the outside, uncompromising on security.

Kampela is a project by Alzymologist Oy, a Finnish company exploring the intersection of hardware, software, and digital security. We bring together engineering expertise and open-source principles to build tools that give people more control over their digital assets.

Every Kampela device is built with precision, care, and attention to detail. Our team combines engineering expertise with a hands-on approach to hardware development, carefully considering every component and production stage.
