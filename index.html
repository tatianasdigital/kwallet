How it works

  1. Step 01

    Generate transaction QR using your non-custodial wallet

  2. Step 02

    Scan the QR using Siltti - Kampela's companion app

  3. Step 03

    Pass transaction data to Kampela using uni-directional NFC

  4. Step 04

    Review transaction metadata and sign it on your Kampela

  5. Step 05

    Sign the transaction with the signature QR on Kampela's screen
