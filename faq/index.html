General

Kampela is a hardware signing device designed to securely store private keys and sign transactions for the Polkadot and Substrate ecosystems. It keeps sensitive cryptographic operations separate from your everyday computer or smartphone, allowing you to review and approve transactions on the device itself.

Kampela works alongside a compatible wallet or dApp and the Siltti companion application. First, you generate a transaction in your wallet. Siltti transfers the transaction data to Kampela via NFC. You can then review the transaction on the device's e-Ink display and approve it. Kampela generates a QR code containing the signature, which can be scanned by your wallet to complete the process.

No. Kampela has no built-in battery. It receives the power it needs through NFC, so there is no need to charge it or worry about battery degradation during storage.

No. Kampela has no Wi-Fi or Bluetooth connectivity. Transaction data is transferred through unidirectional NFC, while signed transactions are exported as QR codes. This design limits the available communication channels and helps isolate private keys from internet-connected devices.

No. Kampela does not store cryptocurrency or manage account balances. Your digital assets remain on their respective blockchains, while Kampela securely stores your private keys and uses them to sign transactions. You remain in control of your assets at all times.

Kampela is designed for the Polkadot and Substrate ecosystems. It supports cryptographic operations for compatible Substrate-based networks, including Polkadot and Kusama. Network compatibility depends on the supported signing protocols and metadata.

Kampela is developed with an open-source approach. Its hardware and software designs are intended to be publicly available, allowing developers and the community to inspect, understand, and contribute to the project. You can explore the available source code and documentation on our GitHub.

No. Firmware updates are intentionally disabled in Kampela's current design. This is a deliberate security decision intended to reduce the risks associated with modifying the device's software after manufacturing.

Other questions

Your cryptocurrency remains on the blockchain, even if you lose your Kampela. However, you may lose access to the private keys stored on the device. Make sure you securely back up your recovery phrase during initial setup. It is essential for recovering access to your accounts if your device is lost or damaged.

Kampela is designed to work with compatible wallets and dApps in the Polkadot and Substrate ecosystems. Integration depends on the wallet's support for Kampela's signing workflow and communication protocol.
